# Troubleshooting

Unavailable prices or history indicate missing credentials, unavailable connectivity, rejected schemas or an empty real account. They do not activate a demo feed. A new connector needs 14 complete observed five-minute bars. Model failures are visible; verify endpoint protocol, model and roster without sharing secrets. Stop the server before recovery or backup. A server.lock conflict means another process owns that account.
