# Cockpit UI

The interface has four destinations: Live monitor, Experience, Strategy lab and Settings. Monitor shows account/source status, heads, tools and the latest decision route. Experience separates broker and paper history, exports recorded executions and opens decision evidence. Strategy lab replays uploaded OHLCV data without account mutations. Settings owns risk limits, funded execution controls, connector credentials, model gateway rosters and English/Persian presentation. Empty and unavailable states remain explicit.
