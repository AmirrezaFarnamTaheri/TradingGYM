# Cockpit UI

The interface has exactly three destinations: Live monitor, Experience and Settings. Monitor shows account/source status, heads, tools and the latest decision route. Experience separates broker and paper history and opens recorded decision evidence. Settings owns risk limits, connector credentials, model gateway rosters and English/Persian presentation. Empty and unavailable states remain explicit.
