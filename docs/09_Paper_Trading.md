# Paper trading

Select paper mode in Settings to use a virtual Decimal account driven by actual observed bars. Fees/slippage are modeled, and paper fills are distinct from broker-confirmed history. Exposure, loss, drawdown, stop-loss and persistent cooldown limits apply. Stop preserves positions; emergency flatten closes at last marks and latches the halt.

See [first run](FIRST_RUN.md), [settings and decision evidence](SETTINGS_AND_DECISIONS.md), and [current verification](REBUILD.md).
